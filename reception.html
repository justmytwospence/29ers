Relay-Sat Reception

There is no cell signal on any of these peaks. Comms go through orbital relay satellites — with a one-way light delay measured in minutes, not bars.

BodyOne-way signal delay (from Earth)
Mars3–22 min
Io33–53 min
Venus2–14 min
Vesta9–25 min
Iapetus68–84 min
Earthunder 1 sec
